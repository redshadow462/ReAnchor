Set Up Authenticator

Protect your ReAnchor account with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.

1. Install an authenticator app

Install Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, or another compatible TOTP authenticator app.

2. Add your ReAnchor account

Open your authenticator app and select Add account or +. Then choose Scan a QR code.
